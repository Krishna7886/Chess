Game Instructions & Voice Guide

  • Turn Voice On: Click Voice ON (or say "Activate" in Standby).
  • Move Pieces: Say piece + square: "Knight F3", "Rook C2", "Bishop takes C4", "Castle", or "Queen side castle".
  • Pawn Moves: Simply say the target square: "E4", "D5", or NATO phonetics like "Echo 4", "Charlie 3".
  • Undo Limits: Easy = 5 Undos, Medium = 3 Undos, Hard = 1 Undo.
  • Voice Controls Everywhere: Say "Choose level", "Player vs Computer", "Player vs Player", "Easy/Medium/Hard", "Start match", "Undo", "Flip board", "Restart", "Mute", "Resign", "Dashboard", or "Stop listening".
