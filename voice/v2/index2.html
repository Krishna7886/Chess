Hands-Free Match Flow:
• Say "Activate", "Voice control", or "Start" to begin listening.
• Say piece name + destination: "Rook C2", "Knight F3".
• For Pawns, just say the destination square: "E4" or "D5".
• System Voice Commands:
• Say "Undo", "Take back", or "Go back" to undo.
• Say "Flip board" or "Rotate" to change the view.
• Say "Restart match" or "Reset" to clear the board.
• Say "Easy", "Medium", or "Hard" to change difficulty.
• Say "Stop listening" or "Voice off" to return to Standby.
